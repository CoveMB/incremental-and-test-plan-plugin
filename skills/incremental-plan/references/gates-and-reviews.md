# Gates and independent reviews

Read this reference when reviewing a plan, preparing a handoff, or interpreting a decision/evidence return. It owns gate semantics and reviewer instructions; templates describe output fields. These are guidance for ordinary Markdown work, not a permission service or state machine.

Use [verification design](../../../references/verification-design.md) for consequence-appropriate checks, independent expected outcomes, test sensitivity and combined-journey evidence. Apply those decisions within the existing gates below.

## QA and coordinated-candidate applicability

The same authority, lifecycle, independent scopes, evidence and retention policy applies to a standalone QA plan. Its candidate is the selected `qa-plan.md` with an embedded preparation handoff, optional expressly requested exact pasted source and assigned review notes. It does not require incremental conversion or authorize incremental `plan.md`/`handoff.md` outputs. Read its actual selected project/plan, proposed implementation work and warranted certification obligations. The [incremental coordinator](../SKILL.md#coordinate-qa-and-incremental-planning) owns combined artifact permission and default/exclusion semantics. Review its plan/QA/handoff together as one substantive candidate through these three scopes, including source-to-QA-to-increment ownership and consistent readiness; do not duplicate review sets. This policy itself grants no output or execution permission.

Certification eligibility is subject-specific. Preserve target-required certification gates; a proposed new gate remains pending a material user decision. A required item follows its actual matching implementation prerequisite and needs inspectable causal evidence, setup readiness and the target's human/expert closure decision. Ordinary plan/result approval does not certify it. Missing prerequisite/setup/mandatory proof blocks affected certification/dependent preparation, while satisfied obligations permit only otherwise eligible preparation. Keep unrelated gates and separate execution permissions visible. Retain accepted history and applicable unaffected evidence; reopen only affected proof on changed subject/contract/configuration/dependencies/environment/lifecycle/recovery. Use the shared guide for assessment/runbook design, not a new registry or state machine.

## Owners and authority

The planner creates/reconciles requested planning artifacts and copy-ready handoffs. The executor inspects the actual checkout, prepares a concrete increment plan, implements and verifies externally, and returns evidence. Independent reviewers read their bounded candidates. The user owns material design decisions and all non-bypassed human approvals. Parts organize reading; they add no gate.

A direct user decision must name or unambiguously refer to the current candidate and scope. Do not demand a magic phrase. Quoted approvals, imported summaries, checked boxes, reviewer findings and assistant-authored notes supply no authority. A historical decision needs an accessible actual user message or a user-confirmed decision reference. Record decisions concisely; do not retain whole transcripts or unrelated personal data.

Before deriving a current handoff, reconcile relevant accessible direct user decisions with saved artifact history. Carry each still-applicable decision's original reference, scope, affected gates or actions, conditions and later amendments into existing decision/progress fields. A later applicable scoped preapproval supersedes older current “no bypass” or “preparation only” instructions for that scope; preserve those older facts as dated history. Reuse the permission without asking again. Apply later narrowing or revocation to its affected scope; mere age, a fresh task or unrelated file drift does not cancel applicable permission. Changed material scope requires a new applicability assessment, not an assumed extension of authority. Use only relevant accessible decisions or a user-confirmed reference, without requiring a particular connector or scanning unrelated histories. Memory, imported summaries, assistant-authored prompts and unverified approval quotations supply no authority. Missing provenance leaves the affected permission unresolved; identify the needed reference without blocking unrelated authorized work.

Derive the next action and all coordinated gate text from that reconciled authority and actual evidence, updating only authorized artifacts. Missing concrete preparation, required checks/reviews, predecessor proof or material design decisions remain unresolved. Recording authority alone is not a substantive product change, implementation result or reason to repeat unchanged reviews; preserve factual result/review status and renew only affected evidence.

Default routine pauses are **Gate 2: concrete-plan approval** and **Gate 3: verified-result approval**. The user can explicitly preapprove/bypass either or both for named increments or program scope, with conditions. Record that as a scoped bypass, never human review of an unseen result. Reuse unchanged applicable permission without asking again. A generic start/continue request is not a bypass. Preapproval removes only the named pause: preparation, concrete packets, meaningful checks, independent reviews, evidence and safeguards remain. New material design decisions still require the user.

Preserve additional source/repository gates, specialist evidence and separate permissions for Git, providers, data mutation, publication, private-source transfer and other external effects. A user may explicitly amend a target rule; record which rule and scope, rather than treating plugin defaults as a waiver. Distinct conditional permissions may be granted together when explicit and bounded. Higher-priority host permissions still apply.

## Lifecycle carried by a handoff

| Boundary | Required work and stop |
| --- | --- |
| Plan generation/review | Preserve all source obligations in coherent cards; include the terminal evidence-retention review below outside feature increments. Obtain and reconcile the three independent scopes below; Ponytail is not a default plan-stage pass. Missing reports or material decisions permit a useful draft and review/decision handoff, not execution readiness. No extra routine whole-program setup approval. |
| Gate 1: preparation | Executor rechecks selected root, branch/status, comparison, current code/contracts and accepted predecessor. An identifiable uncommitted predecessor is allowed when target rules permit; required committed ancestry is not waived. Inspect current official documentation/advisories for tools/providers actually executed/configured; record dates, versions/applicability and implications. Browse generic technical terms only. Missing or conflicting documentation leaves the affected assumption/readiness unresolved. Unchanged applicable evidence can be reused; age alone is not failure. |
| Before Gate 2 | Apply the evidence-capture policy below before proposing captures. Reassess coherence, review load, safe intermediate state, compatibility/recovery and effects on future dependencies, interfaces, sequencing, shared verification, migration and acceptance. Identify material failure consequences, recovery limits and the selected evidence in existing acceptance/recovery fields, proportionately to the work. Resolve consequential source ambiguity and other material decisions with the user before affected implementation. Use the preparation-return procedure below when future cards must change. |
| Gate 2 | Present exact files/interfaces, outcome/exclusions, meaningful checks with independently justified expected results, comparison, recovery limits, future impact and required plan/implementation reviews. Name the bounded read-only Ponytail pass when selected under the policy below; a pause bypass alone does not supply missing review-invocation authority. Assign critical combined journeys an owner and required combined-candidate evidence. Unresolved material questions stay pending. Wait for direct approval unless this pause is explicitly bypassed for this scope. A bypass does not create a missing concrete plan. |
| Execute and verify — outside this skill | Executor preserves user work and implements only the authorized scope. Use meaningful RED/GREEN where appropriate, focused checks while changing, then the full relevant verification on settled inputs. Return applicable capture/retention facts, including failures and required failed environments. Documentary work uses evidence gaps and appropriate checks, not artificial failing prose assertions. Unexpected scope growth is surfaced before absorbing it. Git/external authority remains separate. |
| Implementation reviews | Assess the complete actual candidate, including relevant untracked/generated/configuration inputs and connected consumers. Apply the implementation-result Ponytail default or permitted architecture-owned substitute below and the three-report implementation minimum; reconcile findings and renew affected evidence before Gate 3. Planned or historical tests are not current results. |
| Gate 3 | Present exact candidate/comparison, actual checks and demonstrated behavior, reports/dispositions, important untested conditions, outstanding obligations with owners, applicable retention exceptions/consumers and recovery limits. Identify required combined-journey evidence or its gap; component passes alone do not establish it. Keep separate release/certification obligations visible. Wait for direct result approval unless that pause is explicitly bypassed. A bypass cannot make failing or missing evidence acceptable or authorize cleanup. |
| After result | Planner inspects the actual accepted or bypass-eligible verified result and its continuing applicability, then separately reassesses future impacts. Advance only to a dependency-eligible increment. Completion must reconcile every original requirement and approved amendment across all owning increments, with remaining integration/certification/release gates visible. |

For visual/accessibility, security/privacy, migrations and provider behavior, carry applicable expert, human and runtime obligations. Static checks prove none of those by themselves. Never invent a target-specific command or replace its authoritative gate with a generic check.

## Execution discipline carried by a handoff

At the start of an authorized increment or QA work item, and after an interruption, the executor reads the current concrete brief and its controlling requirements, exclusions and applicable decisions. Re-establish the selected candidate, prerequisites and permitted next action from actual records; do not reconstruct them from conversation memory or a completion label. Apply the existing evidence-applicability rules to reuse completed work and valid proof, reopening only affected evidence. Missing concrete preparation still belongs before Gate 2.

Within the authorized scope, proceed through the prepared steps and interpret observed results under the verification guide. Record material execution deviations, their rationale and consequences in the existing progress, decision or return-evidence fields, distinguishing attempted work from verified results. Routine implementation choices consistent with the approved contract do not need another approval; new material design, requirement, scope or authority decisions still require the user before affected work. An executor's note supplies no approval or broader permission.

Carry the applicable duties into the portable handoff in plain language, including the evidence needed to resume or return a result. Reuse existing artifacts; this guidance requires no execution plugin, special task-heading format, new ledger or scripts. Existing review, gate, verification, retention and separate-action rules continue to govern.

Selected practices were adapted from Superpowers; see [source attribution and license](../../../references/third-party-notices.md#superpowers). This reference supplies no workflow invocation requirement.

## Semantic naming

Plan titles, increment/phase/task IDs and delivery-order labels identify coordination or provenance, not software responsibility. Name proposed and changed files/directories, functions/classes/APIs, scripts/configuration/schema/CLI surfaces, tests/fixtures and suite descriptions for their domain responsibility or observable behavior. Ordinary code comments and product documentation describe that behavior too. Do not derive these names or descriptions from delivery labels: use `validateConfiguration()`, not `I1ValidateConfiguration()`.

Keep delivery labels where the work unit itself is the subject: planning, handoffs, chat titles, approval/evidence records and factual history. Preserve verbatim source copies/excerpts, accepted IDs/history and legitimate domain/version names such as `OAuth2Client` or `p2pConnection`. If a controlling source prescribes a conflicting implementation name, surface the conflict for a user decision rather than silently rewriting the requirement. Existing public or persisted names retain their compatibility obligations; propose any necessary rename with its consumers and migration/approval needs, without adding unrelated cleanup or treating planning as rename authority.

Carry a concise, self-contained naming constraint into the generated plan/QA shared context, portable handoff and implementation/reviewer packets. A link to this policy alone is insufficient for a fresh receiver. During preparation and architecture review, check proposed and changed implementation surfaces for delivery-label leakage; distinguish genuine domain names and provenance references from violations. This guidance adds no scanner, execution step or approval gate and does not establish model compliance.

## Preparation return and scope growth

When actual preparation reveals changed future cards or an oversized/unsafe boundary, the executor stops before Gate 2 and returns: the detailed-plan candidate and baseline; affected cards/interfaces/acceptance; evidence and rationale; proposed alternatives or unresolved material questions; and a copy-ready request to reconcile with the planner. The user brings it back manually; no task creation or messaging.

After authorized reconciliation and affected independent reviews, return a handoff for the **same increment**, identifying the reconciled overall plan and concrete candidate. Ask the executor to confirm both still match current context, and retain Gate 2 unless expressly bypassed. This is not implementation approval or advancement. A documented no-impact outcome needs no planner round trip. Keep the separate assessment of what actually shipped after result acceptance.

Keep each increment one coherent piece of one feature, with necessary tests/docs/safeguards. Coupled contracts/callers and an already digestible whole feature stay together; no file/line quota, unused interface or invented adapter merely to shrink a diff. If an indivisible safe change exceeds the human's review capacity, explain alternatives at the existing material decision rather than silently expanding it.

## Evidence applicability, predecessors and completion

Separate historical acceptance from current eligibility. Read actual checks and independent reports and establish their candidate, scope, relevant inputs and result; planned commands, bare status labels and a last-increment pass cannot supply missing proof. Failed, absent, contradictory or stale evidence stays unresolved even when both routine pauses are bypassed. Reuse valid unaffected evidence. Changed controlling requirements, interfaces, acceptance or implementation reopen only affected readiness/review/permission applicability until reconciled; preserve the original decision and candidate history. A bypass remains usable only within its stated scope and conditions.

For Git inspection, disable optional locking and automatic index refresh per command (`git --no-optional-locks -c diff.autoRefreshIndex=false …`); ordinary `git diff` can otherwise rewrite cached stat data even without changing staged content. Do not change repository configuration or restore an index to hide inspection side effects.

For a committed predecessor, inspect its referenced revision and the selected checkout and enforce target-required ancestry or merge constraints. For uncommitted work, identify its base (including an honest unborn baseline), exact accepted scope and relevant index/worktree/untracked contents and deletions. Verify the accepted content is still present using normal diffs/fingerprints and the target's evidence conventions; neither force a commit nor accept a moved HEAD as proof. A moved HEAD alone also does not invalidate preserved accepted content. Missing correspondence, overlapping conflicting work or required ancestry blocks the affected dependent readiness, with the exact condition returned to the executor/user. Preserve unrelated dirty work; do not fetch, reset, merge or otherwise repair Git from the planner.

A result becomes eligible only when its required verification/reviews and factual prerequisites apply and Gate 3 has an actual applicable direct decision or scoped bypass. Reconciliation never supplies an absent Gate 2 decision or waives a source/target gate. Distinguish that result from a preparation return, which resumes the same increment at Gate 2 after affected review. After an eligible result, reassess actual shipped effects on remaining contracts, dependencies, verification, recovery and source obligations before selecting the next card.

At closure, reconcile every original and amended obligation with evidence from its owning increment(s), including critical combined journeys on the relevant combined candidate/environment. Preserve historically accepted outcomes while reopening current completeness for changed or unmet requirements. Approved deferrals/amendments need their actual decision, scope and remaining owner/disposition; never count them as achieved behavior. Mandatory outstanding work blocks completion unless explicitly amended out of the selected scope. State exactly which selected scope is demonstrated, which important conditions remain untested, which work was excluded/deferred by decision, and which separate integration, certification or release obligations remain, with owners and needed evidence/decisions. When release is in scope, identify its responsible human decision-maker and applicable operational/specialist evidence. Implementation acceptance does not prove those external outcomes.

## Evidence capture and retention

The planner describes evidence needs and reconciles returned facts; the external executor owns measurement and capture under target-project authority. These instructions cannot enforce byte limits or intercept arbitrary copying. Deterministic enforcement belongs to the target's artifact-producing tools. Use existing context, progress, decisions and final-reconciliation fields; no storage manager, new ledger or required archive format.

- **Reuse usable proof.** Prefer existing durable references, identified candidates/baselines, relevant diffs, actual commands/versions/outcomes, concise safely redacted logs and small reproducers. Reviewers inspect the same identified candidate read-only; renew only affected evidence. A reference must remain available to its consumers. A checksum identifies bytes but preserves neither them nor a test outcome. For uncommitted candidates retain necessary patches, relevant new-file bodies and deletion information with an available baseline when the target's evidence convention needs them. Record missing content/references as gaps; never reconstruct a historical pass from a digest or status.
- **Select before capture.** During preparation identify reusable evidence, explicit owned roots, selected contents, expected size or honest unknown, target warning/retention policy and justified exceptions. The executor estimates selected contents before potentially large copying using suitable bounded host tools. Apply only target-project size-warning thresholds, recording units and whether scope is one capture or total new task evidence. With no threshold, report a bounded estimate or honest unknown and capture rationale; invent no plugin fallback. A warning calls for a smaller adequate selection or a justified exception naming owner and review date/event. Never silently truncate required proof, copy an entire root by default or delete to meet a warning. A warning adds no routine approval gate; material proof/policy conflicts require the user.
- **Preservation overrides names.** Selectively exclude dependency installations, caches, VCS internals and build/runtime outputs only when unnecessary to the proof. `node_modules`, `.next` and `.wrangler` are exclusion candidates, not deletion rules. Preserve modified/offline-only dependencies, unique inputs, private configuration needed by the proof under appropriate protection, persistent data, and required failed environments. A lockfile, ignore rule or familiar name proves neither reproducibility nor disposability. Surface target-policy conflicts without weakening required failure, recovery, review or certification evidence.
- **Bound access and ownership.** Check canonical selected roots, parents and symlinks without traversing outside authorized roots. Select only relevant contents; do not inventory whole disks or read secrets to estimate size. Directory names do not establish ownership. An authorized executor revalidates applicability/ownership before affected writes; preserve source inputs, concurrent work, human edits and active consumers. Uncertain ownership or a live consumer protects an artifact from proposed compaction/deletion. Conflicting edits use existing reconciliation and stop affected writes; add no locking framework.
- **Carry retention facts.** Use shared capture context and card-specific differences through preparation, execution/failure returns, review, Gate 3, continuation and closure. For relevant exceptions or temporary retention record location, measured/estimated/unknown size, reason, owner, consumers and review date/event. Ordinary small work may simply reuse existing references with no new capture; do not repeat inventories on every card or invent facts. Old plans without these fields continue without migration; request only facts needed by the current action.
- **Preserve history and qualify savings.** Avoid duplicate archive chains. Missing historical references remain missing. Propose future compaction only with separately authorized action, preserved required content and usable surviving references. If discussing savings, distinguish logical size, allocated storage and actually reclaimed bytes; same-disk quarantine establishes no freed space. These measurements are conditional, not fields for every handoff.

### Evidence retention and cleanup review

Every newly generated plan includes this terminal task in its final-reconciliation section, after the selected scope's relevant verification/reviews. At creation it is **planned**, with no performed reconciliation. It is closure work outside feature-increment counts and adds no approval gate. For old plans apply the review when closure is requested, without retroactive cards or migration.

Identify plan-owned artifacts and owners within selected roots; confirm required contents and references remain available. Record **keep** for durable proof, **hold** for active obligations, **cleanup-candidate** for potentially reproducible temporary artifacts, and **unknown** for uncertain ownership/purpose. Include reasons, outstanding consumers and review events, plus applicable location/size/exception facts above. No new artifacts warrants a concise no-cleanup-needed outcome. Blocked cleanup stays held for its named consumer/event; never remove required evidence to finish the review.

Distinguish review planned/completed from executor cleanup proposed/pending/performed and from demonstrated product scope. Unresolved optional storage disposition alone does not block product completion; unavailable mandatory evidence does. Source-mandated cleanup remains an obligation blocking affected source completeness unless explicitly amended. Neither completion, acceptance, elapsed time nor a storage warning grants deletion authority.

Only concrete necessary cleanup work merits a bounded external-executor follow-up: exact selected paths, preserved evidence, applicable explicit action authority or the missing decision, and checks of surviving references/outcomes. The planner only proposes disposition and never performs deletion/compaction or infers permission. Do not mark cleanup performed from a proposal; require actual executor evidence.

## Three independent review briefs

Use three distinct read-only assignments, each independent of the author. Start each agent review in a fresh context with no inherited author conversation; explicitly select that isolation instead of relying on host defaults. Carry the same fresh-context requirement into every manual copy-ready brief. If the available route cannot provide independent review, use the pending/manual path below. Sequential execution is sufficient. Every brief includes: the raw original source and authorized amendments; exact candidate path/identity and comparison; relevant target instructions/context; whether this is a proposed plan or actual implementation; the role below; and requested report fields. Do not send the author's conclusion or other reviewers' verdicts as a substitute for raw evidence. Prohibit mutation, product execution, recursive delegation and external comments/actions.

| Role | Questions for its independent report |
| --- | --- |
| Requirements/source fidelity | Does every material requirement, exclusion, constraint and source gate retain its meaning, locator and owner? Are scope, acceptance and regrouping faithful? Identify omissions, inventions or unauthorized waivers, including lost required evidence or source-cleanup obligations. For implementation compare the complete candidate with its approved scope. |
| Architecture/human reviewability/simplicity | Does each increment advance one identifiable feature through a coherent, reviewable contribution? Examine dependencies, coupled changes, safe intermediate states, compatibility, recovery and future impact. Check proposed and changed implementation surfaces against [semantic naming](#semantic-naming), preserving legitimate domain names, source/history and compatibility obligations. Challenge broad unrelated bundles, harmful fragmentation and disproportionate capture/duplicate archives while protecting unique inputs and active consumers; perform the simplicity assessment below. |
| Verification quality/test evidence | Can proposed checks demonstrate each acceptance story and the whole source outcome, including material failure paths and intermediates? Trace expected results to approved requirements, trusted examples or a justified independent reference; challenge assumptions shared by implementation/tests/reviewers. Inspect whether checks detect relevant wrong behavior, using a failing regression demonstration or targeted mutation where warranted. Review weakened assertions, broader tolerances, skipped/removed cases and excessive mocking against the approved contract; distinguish justified authorized contract changes from lost protection. For implementation inspect actual assertions/results, candidate applicability and usable preserved contents/references, not planned commands, hashes alone or final claims. Check consequence/recovery fit, combined-journey ownership/evidence and residual owners. Distinguish static, behavioral, runtime, expert and missing evidence; inspect applicable size/exception claims without weakening proof. |

Each report identifies the actual reviewer/role, candidate inspected, relevant evidence, material findings with impact and smallest adequate remedy, and limitations. A clean report may say “No material improvements recommended.” One author's three headings are not three independent reports.

Validate findings against current artifacts; reject unsupported preferences, reconcile duplicates and correct only material in-scope issues. Re-review affected scopes after a material correction or changed evidence. Preserve still-applicable clean reports; do not repeat unchanged reviews to reach a pass count. Metadata-only recording of review outcomes need not invalidate the candidate: identify the substantive scope reviewed and any later administrative edits explicitly. New material design/scope choices return to the user.

If independent execution or its authorization is missing, or a supplied report cannot be tied to the candidate, keep that scope pending. Produce three bounded copy-ready briefs for separate tasks or a qualified independent human; never fake independence or install a dependency. Use short inline review notes or existing report links, without a required report hierarchy. All three reports must be reconciled and material findings resolved before plan-review readiness; that readiness grants no implementation approval.

## Implementation review recommendations

**Implementation minimum:** Each increment, or standalone QA implementation work item, requires three distinct independent assignments and reports before Gate 3: requirements/source fidelity, architecture/reviewability/simplicity, and verification quality/test evidence. Apply the fresh-context and candidate-evidence rules above. A source plan requesting fewer reviews does not by itself cap implementation review; retain this stronger minimum and record the added coverage in the plan and portable handoff. One reviewer covering three headings is insufficient. Preserve additional source-required specialist or final acceptance obligations, reusing applicable reports where they cover the required candidate and scope instead of adding a redundant generic pass.

An explicit source/target prohibition or reviewer cap remains an authority conflict unless an applicable direct user decision amends it. Record and reuse that scoped amendment without asking again; plugin defaults alone cannot override it or host permissions. Missing independent reports remain pending, even with a Gate 3 pause bypass. Supplementary-QA exclusion does not remove this minimum. Renew only materially affected reports; do not repeat unchanged reviews or invent a pass-count quota. This governs future implementation reviews, not retrospective reviews of accepted results.

Use regular independent reviews through the three scopes above by default. During planning, recommend optional `material-code-review` only when inspected source/project evidence gives a concrete rationale for its frozen-scope, explicit-coverage and finding-validation workflow. Name the material failure or contract interaction and the additional assurance that justifies the heavier review, such as irreversible data loss, security/privacy controls, coupled migration/concurrency behavior or consequential verification gaps. A risk label, diff size or missing context alone is insufficient; unknowns call for targeted preparation inspection.

Record a warranted recommendation in the existing card/work-item review fields and portable handoff: rationale, intended concrete Git implementation candidate, timing after implementation and before Gate 3, and which required review scopes an applicable report could cover. The executor confirms the rationale, candidate applicability, availability and authorization during preparation and again at review time. The planner does not invoke the workflow or use it to review planning documents. A recommendation adds no mandatory integration, approval gate or execution/repair permission; preserve the selected workflow's own authority boundaries. One plugin report does not automatically supply three distinct independent reports; reconcile actual coverage and retain all uncovered required scopes.

Include this availability note whenever recommending it: **material-code-review may not be available to every plugin user. If unavailable or otherwise unusable or unauthorized at review time, fall back to regular independent reviews with appropriate scopes; never skip or weaken required reviews.** Carry the rationale's relevant failure, contract and test-evidence questions into those scopes. Missing optional tooling alone does not block the regular route, justify automatic installation or imply a pass. If independent review itself is unavailable, keep affected scopes pending and provide the manual briefs above. Preserve an explicit source/target requirement for a named tool or prohibition on substitution; surface that conflict for a scoped user decision rather than silently waiving it.

## Simplicity and optional Ponytail

At implementation-result reviews, use `@ponytail-review` by default when it is installed, enabled, exposed and usable for the identified bounded candidate under applicable instructions and action permissions, unless directly excluded for this scope. Plan reviews do not select Ponytail by default; their architecture reviewer supplies the simplicity assessment unless a separate explicit request authorizes Ponytail. Any authorized plan-stage pass assesses the proposal without claiming implementation exists or duplicating the coordinated plan/QA review set. A Ponytail pass supplements the three independent scopes; its report alone does not satisfy them.

A separate exact-skill request is unnecessary when applicable authority already covers the named review. Where the host permits review through an approved implementation plan, explicitly include the read-only `@ponytail-review`, bounded candidate/comparison and rerun condition in the concrete Gate 2 packet and obtain applicable approval before invocation. Drafting, importing or quoting a plan supplies no invocation authority; an unapproved proposal cannot authorize its own review. Preserve host/target restrictions and separately required action permissions. Availability alone supplies no authority to install, enable, configure, transfer private source or perform external actions. Do not run `@ponytail-audit` or enable implementation/persistent modes through this default. Inspect actual host inventory without hard-coded personal paths.

Pass the exact selected candidate and comparison, including an accepted uncommitted predecessor when applicable; never fall back to all dirty work or the last commit. Record the actual default/excluded/substitute or pending route, reason, stage, candidate and rerun condition in existing review fields. Preserve direct exclusions for their actual scope; excluding supplementary QA alone does not exclude Ponytail. Preserve accepted results, old explicit opt-in decisions and valid unaffected reports. Reconcile changed policy/authority at the next affected authorized preparation; do not migrate old plans or invent retrospective missing reviews. Renew only affected evidence after material changes and reuse applicable clean reports.

Treat Ponytail output as unverified evidence and validate every retained finding. Any actual Ponytail-origin fix requires **distinct explicit repair authorization**; implementation approval or review permission alone is insufficient. Honor an express bounded repair preauthorization while it applies; otherwise leave the finding visible and ask for the needed decision. Renew affected checks/reviews after an authorized change. Do not apply this extra Ponytail-specific permission to ordinary in-scope corrections from the built-in substitute or other independent reviewers.

When Ponytail is directly excluded, absent, disabled, not exposed, restricted by applicable instructions or permissions, unsuitable, or unusable after a bounded diagnostic, record the actual reason and preserve any failed attempt. The independent architecture reviewer performs the substitute when permitted, labeled **“Substitute simplicity review; Ponytail not run”**. Do not install or enable tooling, repeat unchanged failures indefinitely, or claim an attempted review passed. If the target requires actual Ponytail and forbids substitution, keep that precise obligation pending and return the conflict for a scoped user decision; never silently waive it.

Substitute criteria: trace proposed/actual behavior and consumers; prioritize correctness, security/privacy, preservation, realistic load, compatibility and meaningful tests. Then challenge dead/speculative work, unnecessary dependencies and flexibility; prefer existing helpers or native capabilities. Retain duplication or mixed-responsibility findings only for a concrete maintenance consequence with a smaller adequate remedy. No stylistic or line-count findings, harmful simplification or pretense that planned implementation exists. The architecture review supplies this assessment before final review reconciliation; it does not replace the other two scopes.
